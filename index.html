Welcome to Syed Farm House

Syed Farm House is a growing farmhouse business established in 2022, focused on premium-quality livestock including hens, cows, sheep, goats, Aligarh & Kangayam bulls, camels, Marchalla goats, ostriches and pigeons.

Our presentation is simple by design: healthy farm animals, responsible care, clear communication and a warm relationship with the farming community.
