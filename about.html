About the farm

A farmhouse built around quality

Syed Farm House (SFH) was established in 2022 with a straightforward focus: premium farm animals, responsible care and useful communication for farmers and customers.

Our story

From a local farm to a trusted identity

Syed Farm House brings together poultry, cattle, draught bulls, camel, goat, ostrich and pigeon categories in one clear farm identity. The business is led by Proprietor Syedsha.

The farm's website is intentionally practical. It avoids unsupported breed, pricing or certification claims and keeps availability information editable, so customers can contact the farm for current details.

What matters to us

Farm values

Quality

We present quality as a working principle and keep exact animal or breed claims open for confirmation.

Animal Care

Responsible farm management and attention to animal care are central to the farm experience.

Trust

Clear contact information and direct enquiries help farmers and customers make informed decisions.

Our approach

Simple, responsible, farmer-focused

SFH is designed to make farm enquiries easy: choose an animal category, review the available information and contact the farm directly for current details.
